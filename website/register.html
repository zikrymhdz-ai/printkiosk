Terms & Conditions

This explains what information PrintKiosk collects when you create an account, how it is used and protected, and your rights. Please read it carefully.

1. What information we collect

When you register we ask for your full name, Gmail address, Malaysian phone number, a password of your choice, and (optionally) face data and a 4-digit PIN for fast kiosk login. When you use the service we store your uploaded print files, print settings, coin transactions, profile picture, and username.

2. Why we collect it

Your Gmail and phone number are used to identify your account, verify your email address during registration, and contact you about your orders. Your password lets only you access your account. Face data and PIN are optional conveniences so you can pick up prints at the shop without typing. Your print files and settings exist for one reason: so the shop can print and deliver your documents. Coins and transactions let you earn and use rewards with your account.

3. Where and how it is stored

All data is stored securely in the cloud using Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL database with private file storage) and served by our Netlify website. Passwords are hashed (scrambled) with bcrypt so they are never stored or read in plain text. Your print files are kept in private cloud storage, not on public servers. Your login session is stored only on your own device.

4. Who can access it

Only you and the shop owner / administrator who operates PrintKiosk can view your information, and only to complete your bookings, print your files, and provide support. Shop staff may see the files, PIN, and name needed to deliver your prints. No one else - members of the public, other customers, or advertisers - can see your data.

5. How long we keep it

Email verification codes expire after 10 minutes and password reset links after 30 minutes. Print files are kept until your job is printed and completed, then deleted from storage. Your account and profile data are kept while your account is active. You can close your account at any time by requesting deletion, which removes your personal data, print jobs, and transaction history.

6. Sharing with third parties

We never sell or share your information for marketing. We use trusted service providers purely to run the service: Supabase hosts our database and files, Netlify serves the website and backend, and Gmail sends verification and password-reset emails. These providers process data only on our behalf and cannot use it for their own purposes.
